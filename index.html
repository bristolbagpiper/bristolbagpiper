Former Scots Guards piper with exceptional experience

Julian Smart brings more than 40 years of piping experience, including his time with the Scots Guards, high-profile ceremonial duties and 20 years as Pipe Major of the City of Bristol Pipes and Drums, with travel across Bristol, the surrounding area and parts of Wiltshire.

Services

Professional performance, thoughtful tune choices and reliable support from first enquiry to the event itself.

Weddings

Piping for arrivals, guest welcome, ceremony moments and receptions, with help choosing tunes that suit the day.

Funerals & memorials

Sensitive, respectful piping for funerals, wakes and memorial services, including traditional laments and family requests.

Parties & ceremonies

A striking live performance for birthdays, civic events, military occasions and other gatherings that call for something memorable.

Experience and background

Julian has more than 40 years of experience as a piper, including his time with the Scots Guards, major ceremonial duties and 20 years as Pipe Major of the City of Bristol Pipes and Drums.

  • More than 40 years of piping experience.

  • 20 years as Pipe Major of the City of Bristol Pipes and Drums.
